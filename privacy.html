Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains how Bajju VC handles information for video conference coordination, portal access and Telegram alerts. It applies to the GitHub Pages portal and its connected Google Apps Script backend.

Who operates this service

Bajju VC is administered by Aditya Narayan Swami, Programmer, Bajju, for local VC coordination. It is a separate coordination tool that uses RajVC scheduling information; it is not the RajVC portal. For privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests, contact answami99@gmail.com. The associated Google consent configuration may display the existing app name “Dashboard”.

Information we handle

  • VC records: meeting titles, departments, dates, times, chairpersons, participants, room locations, status, and manually entered meeting links or order references.
  • Officer directory: names, departments, mobile numbers and active or deleted status supplied by authorized users.
  • Access records: the name assigned to a reusable key, role, key records, browser registration identifiers and secrets, browser/device labels, and creation and last-use times. Keys can be viewed by the host, so they are not stored solely as irreversible hashes. We do not collect MAC addresses.
  • Telegram subscriptions: chat identifiers, names and usernames supplied by Telegram, recorded joining times where available, subscription state and notification delivery records. Chat identifiers are not mobile numbers; subscribing does not itself disclose a phone number to this bot.
  • Operational records: review selections, message preparation or recorded sending status, check times, durations, errors and activity history.

Google account and spreadsheet data

The administrator authorizes Google Apps Script to read and update the configured Google spreadsheet and run the coordination service. Spreadsheet data includes the schedules, officer directory, reviews, settings and operational logs described above. Google account identity may be used for owner authorization. Portal visitors using access keys do not grant access to their personal Google Drive merely by opening the portal. The GitHub frontend communicates with the administrator’s Apps Script backend; it does not directly log in to the visitor’s Google Sheets.

Google data is used to provide the visible scheduling, review, contact management and notification features. Authorized portal users can view data needed for those features; host-only access management and administration are restricted by role. The administrator can inspect the configured sheet and records for operation, support and security.

Bajju VC follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, when using or transferring information received from Google APIs. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it for unrelated profiling.

Why we use information

We use these records to retrieve relevant schedules, manage manually entered VCs, identify changes and cancellations, allow authorized people to review meetings and select recipients, prepare messages, operate subscriptions and troubleshoot failures. Officer selections are manual; sending a message is not used to learn automatic department suggestions.

Messages and third-party services

  • Google and GitHub: Google hosts the backend, spreadsheet and script storage; GitHub Pages hosts the frontend and these public pages. Private spreadsheet contents are not intentionally published in the GitHub repository.
  • RajVC and Cloudflare: RajVC schedule requests pass through the existing Cloudflare relay. The relay is used for schedule retrieval, not for broadcasting the officer directory.
  • Telegram: eligible VC details, including meeting departments and participants when available, are sent to active bot subscribers. Subscribers receive common BHQ alerts. Do not enter confidential material into a meeting intended for broadcast.
  • SMS and WhatsApp: when an authorized user approves messages, the selected mobile numbers and message bodies are passed to the device’s SMS Shortcut/Messages workflow or WhatsApp links. Those services and the mobile carrier process messages under their own policies. A recorded “sent” status is not independent proof of delivery.

These providers may process connection information such as IP addresses and service logs under their own policies. Information may be processed outside India depending on provider infrastructure. The app does not add advertising trackers or sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or necessary to investigate security incidents.

Browser storage and access

The browser stores registration information and cryptographic material in its website storage so you can return without entering your key again. Temporary SMS handoff records are also stored locally. Clearing website data or using a different browser can require entering the key again. Access keys remain reusable until the host deletes them; the host can revoke keys or browser access. Do not share host keys or access to your device. Public policy pages do not require an access key.

Retention, correction and removal

There is no general automatic retention period currently configured. Records remain until the administrator cleans them up or removes them. Deleting an officer in the portal marks the record deleted and may retain the row and related history; it is not a permanent erasure of every copy. Deleting an access key revokes its use but may leave operational history. Telegram /stop stops alerts; it does not necessarily erase subscription history.

Contact the administrator to request access to, correction of, or permanent removal of your information, or to ask about retained records. Requests may require identity verification. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or security, and provider backups or logs may remain according to their own retention rules. The administrator can revoke the app’s Google authorization through Google account settings.

Safeguards and changes

The service uses HTTPS, signed browser requests and role checks, but reusable access keys and third-party dependencies have limitations. No service can guarantee uninterrupted availability or absolute security. This tool is intended for adult office coordination, not for children. This page will show the date of policy revisions; new uses of Google data will require appropriate notice and consent before being introduced.
