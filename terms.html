Terms of service

Effective and last updated: 8 October 2026

These terms describe authorized use of Bajju VC, a locally administered coordination desk for video conference schedules and notifications.

Purpose and operator

The service is administered by Aditya Narayan Swami, Programmer, Bajju. It helps users review RajVC schedules, enter VCs received through other orders, select officers, prepare SMS or WhatsApp messages, and receive Telegram alerts. It is separate from RajVC and does not replace an official meeting order or communication from the organizing department.

Authorized access

The host issues reusable access keys and assigns portal roles. A key may be used in multiple browsers until the host deletes it; saved browser access depends on website storage remaining available. The host may revoke access when necessary. Keep keys and devices private, do not attempt to bypass role checks, and notify the administrator if you suspect unauthorized access.

Your responsibilities

Enter accurate officer numbers and meeting information, and use contact details only where you are authorized to coordinate official work. Verify dates, times, participants, cancellations and recipients against the relevant order before sending. Avoid posting confidential or unnecessary personal information in fields that may be broadcast. Do not use the service for spam, harassment, unlawful content or interference with the service or its providers.

Messages and alerts

Telegram subscriptions provide eligible BHQ VC updates to active subscribers. Use /stop to stop alerts. Portal users approve recipient selection and message preparation. SMS relies on the configured Apple Shortcut and Messages permissions; WhatsApp uses the existing recipient-chat workflow. Device confirmation may be required. A completed Shortcut or recorded sending status does not guarantee recipient delivery or reading.

Scheduled checks are intended to run about every 15 minutes in IST, from 08:30 to 17:00 for the current day and 17:30 to 21:00 for the following day. Hosting limits, source delays, network errors or authorization issues may delay checks or notifications. Alerts are intended to avoid repeats, but duplicate, delayed or missing alerts can occur. Use official communication channels for urgent or critical coordination.

Availability and charges

The app currently has no service subscription charge. Your internet provider, mobile carrier or messaging provider may charge for data or SMS. Google, GitHub, Cloudflare, Telegram, Apple, WhatsApp and RajVC are independent dependencies with their own terms and policies. The administrator may maintain, change or pause the service; continuous operation and schedule accuracy cannot be guaranteed.

Privacy and retained records

Read the privacy policy for the information processed, authorized sharing, browser storage and removal options. Portal deletion or unsubscribing may not permanently erase operational history or backups. Contact the administrator for correction or removal requests.
