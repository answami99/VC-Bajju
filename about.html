Schedules, reviews and communication

The desk combines a GitHub Pages interface with a Google Apps Script backend and a configured Google spreadsheet. The administrator authorizes Google access for that spreadsheet. Visitors use host-issued access keys; they do not need to grant the desk access to their personal Google Drive.

Telegram subscribers receive eligible BHQ schedule alerts. Authorized portal users can select officers and approve SMS or WhatsApp messages through their device workflows. Automatic checks are scheduled about every 15 minutes: 08:30–17:00 for today and 17:30–21:00 for tomorrow, in IST.
